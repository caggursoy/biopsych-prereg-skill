Open tooling for reproducible biopsychology

Write your preregistration before you see the data.

A structured builder for EEG/ERP, fMRI, online behavioral and psychophysiology studies. Pick a modality, answer the sections, download a PREREGISTRATION_DRAFT.md that follows the community templates this repository already ships. Everything runs in your browser — no install, no account, no server.

Four modalities, one workflow

Each modality carries its own acquisition parameters, preprocessing plan and analysis plan, plus the metadata and ethics sections every preregistration needs.

How it works

1. Choose a modality

The builder loads the section list and field ranges from the repository's own configuration files.

2. Fill the sections

Required fields are marked. Anything you leave blank is written into the draft as [TO BE COMPLETED] rather than silently dropped.

3. Review and export

Preview the assembled Markdown, then download it, copy it, or print to PDF. Your answers stay in this browser.

Optional AI assistance

If you have a Groq API key, the builder can expand a terse answer into full prose one section at a time. It is off by default, it never invents parameters, and the free tier's rate limits are enforced client-side so you can see exactly why a request is waiting.

No key is bundled with this site. You supply your own, it is stored only in this browser's local storage, and it is sent only to api.groq.com. The builder works fully without it.

Design

Built to Dieter Rams' ten principles

The interface follows the design discipline Dieter Rams set out at Braun, and which he summarised as less, but better. It is stated here openly rather than left as an unspoken influence: neutral paper, hairline rules, one signal colour, tabular numerals, and no shadow, gradient or ornament that does not carry information.

Good design is innovative

The form is generated from the repository's own templates and configs, so the tool changes when the science does, not when a trend does.

Good design makes a product useful

Every control earns its place: pick a modality, answer the sections, export. Nothing is present for decoration.

Good design is aesthetic

One signal colour, one type family, one grid. Restraint is what makes the draft, not the chrome, the thing you look at.

Good design makes a product understandable

Four numbered steps, labelled fields, and a progress count that says exactly how many required fields remain.

Good design is unobtrusive

The tool is a neutral surface. It holds your preregistration without competing with it for attention.

Good design is honest

Blank required fields are exported as [TO BE COMPLETED], never filled in silently. Rate limits are shown before they block you.

Good design is long-lasting

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no framework and no build step at request time. It will still load in a decade.

Good design is thorough down to the last detail

Tabular numerals so counters do not jitter, visible focus rings, real labels, and a countdown on the token budget.

Good design is environmentally friendly

No analytics, no third-party scripts, no CDN, no tracking. The whole site is under half a megabyte and runs offline.

Good design is as little design as possible

Less, but better. Hairlines instead of shadows, one accent instead of a palette, and no element that could be removed without loss.

Principles as formulated by Dieter Rams. This site is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Dieter Rams, his estate, or Braun.
