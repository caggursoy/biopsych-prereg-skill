# Ethics and Data Privacy Template for Biopsychology Research

## Purpose

This template provides guidance on documenting ethical considerations and data privacy practices for biopsychology preregistrations. These considerations apply across all modalities.

## Ethical Approval

### IRB/Ethics Committee
- **Ethics Approval Status**: 
  - Approved
  - Pending
  - Not yet submitted
  - Exempt
- **Ethics Committee Name**: Full name of reviewing body
- **Approval Number**: Reference number from ethics committee
- **Approval Date**: Date of approval
- **Expiration Date**: When approval expires (if applicable)
- **Amendment History**: Any modifications to original approval

### Informed Consent
- **Consent Procedure**: How consent will be obtained
  - Written consent
  - Electronic consent
  - Verbal consent (with documentation)
- **Consent Form Language**: Language(s) in which consent is provided
- **Consent for Data Sharing**: Separate consent for data sharing (Yes/No)
- **Consent for Future Use**: Permission for future research use
- **Withdrawal Rights**: How participants can withdraw and what happens to their data

### Vulnerable Populations
- **Special Populations**: If study involves:
  - Minors (children/adolescents)
  - Pregnant women
  - Prisoners
  - Cognitively impaired individuals
  - Other vulnerable groups
- **Additional Protections**: Special safeguards for vulnerable populations
- **Assent Procedures**: For minors or those with limited capacity

## Data Privacy and Protection

### Personal Data Collection
- **Types of Personal Data Collected**:
  - Name
  - Date of birth
  - Contact information
  - Demographic information
  - Health information
  - Genetic information
  - Biometric data (facial images, voice recordings)
  - Other (specify)

### Data Anonymization
- **Anonymization Method**:
  - Direct identifiers removed
  - Pseudonymization (coded)
  - Full anonymization
- **Linking Key**: 
  - Will a linking key be maintained? (Yes/No)
  - Who has access to the linking key?
  - How long will the linking key be retained?
- **Re-identification Risk**: Assessment of re-identification risk
- **Mitigation Strategies**: Steps to minimize re-identification

### Data Storage and Security

#### During Data Collection
- **Storage Location**: Where data is stored during collection
- **Access Control**: Who has access to raw data
- **Encryption**: Is data encrypted? (Yes/No, method)
- **Backup Procedures**: How data is backed up

#### Long-term Storage
- **Repository**: Where data will be stored long-term
- **Retention Period**: How long data will be kept
- **Destruction Plan**: How and when data will be destroyed
- **Archive Location**: Institutional or public archive

### Data Sharing

#### Public Data Sharing
- **Will Data Be Shared Publicly?**: Yes/No
- **Repository**: Where data will be shared (e.g., OSF, OpenNeuro, PsychArchives)
- **Level of Sharing**:
  - Raw data
  - Preprocessed data
  - Summary statistics only
  - Statistical maps/results only
- **Embargo Period**: Delay before public release (if any)
- **License**: Data use license (e.g., CC0, CC-BY)

#### Restricted Data Sharing
- **Controlled Access**: Will some data require approval for access?
- **Access Procedure**: How researchers can request access
- **Data Use Agreement**: Required agreements for data use

### Compliance

#### Regulatory Compliance
- **GDPR Compliance**: For EU participants (Yes/No/N/A)
- **HIPAA Compliance**: For US health data (Yes/No/N/A)
- **Other Regulations**: Country-specific data protection laws

#### Institutional Policies
- **Data Management Plan**: Link to or summary of DMP
- **Institutional Review**: Required institutional approvals
- **Data Transfer Agreements**: For multi-site studies

## Special Considerations for Biopsychology

### Neuroimaging Data
- **Incidental Findings Protocol**: 
  - Will scans be reviewed for incidental findings?
  - Who will review?
  - Procedure for communicating findings to participants
- **Facial Features**: 
  - Will facial features be removed from structural scans?
  - Method for defacing/skull-stripping
- **Genetic Information**: 
  - If genetic data collected, special consent required
  - Storage and sharing restrictions

### Psychophysiological Data
- **Health Information**: 
  - ECG, EDA, or other physiological data may reveal health conditions
  - Participant notification procedures
- **Video/Audio Recordings**:
  - Consent for recording
  - Storage and sharing restrictions
  - Retention period

### Online/Remote Data Collection
- **IP Address Collection**: Will IP addresses be collected?
- **Geolocation Data**: Will location data be collected?
- **Third-Party Platforms**: 
  - Privacy policies of platforms used (e.g., Qualtrics, Prolific)
  - Data processing agreements
- **International Data Transfer**: 
  - Will data cross international borders?
  - Safeguards for international transfer

## Participant Rights

### Information Provided to Participants
- **Purpose of Research**: Clear explanation of study goals
- **Procedures**: What participants will be asked to do
- **Risks and Benefits**: Potential risks and benefits
- **Confidentiality**: How privacy will be protected
- **Voluntary Participation**: Right to decline or withdraw
- **Contact Information**: Who to contact with questions or concerns

### Participant Compensation
- **Compensation Amount**: Payment or other compensation
- **Compensation Method**: How participants will be paid
- **Partial Completion**: Payment for partial participation
- **Tax Implications**: Information about tax reporting (if applicable)

### Debriefing
- **Debriefing Procedure**: How participants will be debriefed
- **Deception**: If deception used, how it will be revealed
- **Resources**: Mental health or other resources provided (if applicable)

## Notes

- Consult with your institution's IRB/ethics committee for specific requirements
- Data privacy laws vary by country and region - ensure compliance with all applicable regulations
- For multi-site studies, ensure compliance with regulations in all locations
- Update this section if ethical approval or data management plans change
- Consider cultural sensitivities and local norms in international research
